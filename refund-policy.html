Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 2024

1. Refund Eligibility

Million Education offers refunds under the following conditions:

  • Within 7 Days: Full refund if no classes have been conducted
  • Before First Class: Full refund if cancelled before your first session
  • After First Class: Refund for unused sessions, minus 10% cancellation fee
  • Emergency: Full refund with appropriate documentation

2. Non-Refundable Items

  • Completed sessions or consumed classes
  • Promotional discounts or coupon codes
  • Registration and administrative fees

3. Refund Process

To request a refund:

  1. Contact support@millioneducation.com with your enrollment details
  2. Provide a reason for your refund request
  3. Our team reviews your request within 3-5 business days
  4. If approved, refunds are processed within 7-10 business days to your original payment method

4. Cancellation & Rescheduling

Session Cancellation: Cancel with at least 24 hours notice without penalty. Less than 24 hours may result in the session being marked as "attended".

Rescheduling: Free rescheduling allowed with 48 hours notice. Up to 2 sessions per batch can be rescheduled without charges.

5. Special Circumstances

  • Tutor Mismatch: Free replacement available. If unsatisfied after one replacement, 50% partial refund offered
  • Technical Issues: Makeup classes or credits provided at no charge
  • Service Failure: Full refund may be granted at our discretion

6. Payment Processing

Refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5-10 business days. International transactions may take longer.
